# Concept: index and log

Two special files: content catalog vs chronological ops narrative.

## Current synthesis

- **index.md** — catalog with one-liners; update every ingest.
- **log.md** — append-only `## [date] op | title` lines.
- At moderate scale, index-first beats pure RAG over raw dumps
  ([sources/index-first.md](../sources/index-first.md)).

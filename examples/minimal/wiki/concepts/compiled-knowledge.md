# Compiled knowledge

Knowledge that is **written once into a maintained wiki** rather than re-derived
from raw documents on every query.

## Current synthesis

- Raw sources stay immutable.
- Wiki pages (especially hubs) update on ingest.
- Good queries get filed back.
- Evidence pins raise trust for load-bearing claims — but only over sources that
  came from outside the project.

This synthesis is L1: it restates [karpathy-llm-wiki](../sources/karpathy-llm-wiki.md),
which is own-corpus. That is the honest level for it, and leaving it there costs
nothing.
